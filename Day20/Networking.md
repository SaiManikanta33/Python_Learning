    1.    Networking Basics

    What is a Socket?

    A scoket is an endpoint used for communication between two devices over a network.'
    Think of it like a phone call:
    --> One device listens (Server 📞)
    --> Another device connects (client 📱)


            Important Terms
      Term              Meaning
    - Ip Address        Identifies a device on a network
    - Port              Identifies a specific service on a device
    - Socket             Connection endpoint
    - Client             Initites a connection
    - Server             Waits for incoming connections
